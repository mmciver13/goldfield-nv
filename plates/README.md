# Higgsfield plates

This branch adds a photographic layer to the film. Each plate is a still that dissolves in over the 3D scene for one chapter, drifts slowly, then dissolves back out. The "Plates on / off" button under the Goldfield wordmark switches the layer so the two versions can be compared in place.

## How to add a plate

1. Generate the still in Higgsfield with the prompt below.
2. Export as JPEG, 16:9, at least 2560 px wide, under about 600 KB.
3. Save it in this folder under the exact file name listed.

A plate appears as soon as its file exists. Until then the chapter shows the stand-in photograph where one is listed, otherwise the 3D scene.

Keep every plate free of lettering, logos and recognisable people. Leave the lower-left third dark and quiet: the headline sits there.

Suggested models: Cinema Studio Image 2.5 or Soul Cinema for the landscapes and period scenes; Nano Banana Pro with a reference photo for the Car Forest and the saloon, so they match the real place.

## Prompts

Shared style line, append to every prompt:

> Cinematic still, anamorphic lens, low-key lighting, deep shadows, fine film grain, muted desert palette, photoreal, no text, no logos, no people facing camera, 16:9.

**01-milky-way** — The Milky Way core rising over the high Nevada desert on a moonless night, dense star field, dark dust lanes, faint warm glow at the horizon, black silhouette of low desert ridges along the bottom edge.

**02-car-forest** — Night at the International Car Forest of the Last Church near Goldfield, Nevada: a graffiti-covered bus and several cars planted nose-down in bare desert, faintly light-painted, Orion and the Milky Way overhead, a rounded volcanic hill on the horizon. Use the bus photo as reference.

**03-saloon-exterior** — A small one-storey frontier saloon in 1905 in pre-dawn light: weathered board walls, stepped wooden false front with a faded hand-painted sign area left blank, covered board sidewalk on posts, lamplit windows, pack burros tied at a hitching rail, bare desert and a dark conical mountain behind, a wooden mine headframe in the distance.

**04-saloon-interior** — Inside an old Nevada saloon at night: long dark wooden bar with a columned mirrored back bar, red tufted bar chairs, red patterned carpet, a pool table under a red hanging lamp, a stained-glass playing-card lamp, a wall papered with business cards, a red neon glow from one side. Use the interior photo as reference.

**05-boomtown-1908** — Aerial view of a Nevada gold boomtown in 1908 at sunrise: a wide grid of dirt streets packed with two and three-storey brick and false-front wooden buildings, canvas tents at the edges, wooden headframes and a large stepped stamp mill on the slope of a rust-red conical mountain to the north-east, chimney smoke, long shadows.

**06-main-street-1908** — Street level on a busy boomtown main street in 1908, morning sun: wide dirt street, wooden false-front saloons and brick banks with awnings, telegraph poles, freight wagons, a string of pack burros, figures in period dress seen from behind and at a distance, a four-storey granite and red-brick hotel at the end of the street.

**07-flood-1913** — A flash flood tearing through a desert mining town in 1913 under black storm clouds: brown water filling a dirt street, wooden buildings leaning and breaking apart, driving rain, a single lightning strike, low angle from the water line.

**08-fire-1923** — A desert town's business district burning at dusk in 1923: rows of wooden false-front buildings fully alight, orange flame and heavy black smoke rolling across the frame, charred frames in the foreground, a large brick hotel standing untouched at the edge.

**09-today** — Present-day Goldfield, Nevada in hard afternoon light: an empty highway through a nearly deserted town, a four-storey abandoned granite and brick hotel, a stone courthouse, empty lots of sagebrush, a few weathered houses and trailers, a wild burro crossing the road, pale desert hills.

**10-hotel-night** — The abandoned four-storey Goldfield Hotel at night from the street: granite ground floor, red brick above, columned entrance porch, two upper windows glowing a sickly pale green, thin ground mist, cold blue moonlight, a faint pale figure standing far back in the doorway.

**11-cemetery** — A small pioneer cemetery in open Nevada desert at blue hour: leaning wooden crosses and weathered headstones inside a rail fence, a bare Joshua tree, a wooden gate arch with a blank board, low mist, first stars.

**12-gemfield** — Golden hour turning to dusk in empty high desert: pale blue, green and pink chalcedony stones scattered on bare ground and catching the last light, a lone rockhound in a wide-brimmed hat seen from behind with a bucket and rock hammer, two wild burros, a Joshua tree, first stars appearing above a glowing western horizon.
